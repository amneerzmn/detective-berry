Case File #001

The Missing Fruit Basket

Read the story. Study the clues. What do you think happened?

🔎 Investigation 1

It was a sunny morning in Berry's Garden. Detective Berry was preparing a healthy fruit basket for the Junior Detective Club.

Suddenly, the basket disappeared! Six mysterious clues were left behind: something red and round, something long and yellow, something orange and juicy, a bunch of little fruits, something green-striped, and a fruit with a spiky crown.

“Hmm!” said Berry. “These clues must tell us something. Let's investigate!”

Your mission is to identify the fruits and discover how they can be part of healthy food choices.

Learning Zone

Fruit Detective Academy

Meet the six suspects and learn the English words to describe them.

🍓 Learn the clues
🍎

Apple

Red / green

I am round. My skin can be red or green. I grow on a tree.

💡 Health clue: Apples provide fibre.
🍌

Banana

Yellow

I am long and curved. My peel is usually yellow when I am ripe.

💡 Health clue: Bananas provide potassium.
🍊

Orange

Juicy

I am round and orange. I have a juicy inside.

💡 Health clue: Oranges provide vitamin C.
🍇

Grapes

A bunch

I am made up of small fruits. I can be green, red or purple.

💡 Health clue: Grapes contain water and other nutrients.
🍉

Watermelon

Green stripes

I am large, green outside and usually red inside. I contain plenty of water.

💡 Health clue: Watermelon contributes to hydration.
🍍

Pineapple

Spiky crown

I have a spiky crown and rough skin. My inside is juicy and yellow.

💡 Health clue: Pineapple provides vitamin C.
